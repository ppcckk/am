Gerald Hutchins has been a municipal building inspector for thirty-one years. He has never once been asked to fight a dragon. He has been asked to measure door widths. He has been asked to check egress routes. He has been asked to determine whether a load-bearing wall is, in fact, load-bearing.

Now he is a dungeon core. His dungeon is a 4,000-square-foot sub-basement beneath the Ashford Municipal Library. His spawn list includes three mold spores, a water leak, and a single confused rat. His Compliance Rating is 47 out of 100. He is being fined by the System for inadequate ventilation.

Then he discovers the fine print.

Every dungeon in the System has a hidden Compliance Rating. Every dungeon can be cited for violations. And Gerald Hutchins has been writing citations his entire adult life.

He is going to shut them all down.
