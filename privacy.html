Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Serial Shelf is a free fiction site. This page explains what information is collected when you read here, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

We do not ask you to register, and we do not ask for an email address. Reading requires no account and no login.

Our host records standard server logs for every request: IP address, user agent, requested path, timestamp, and referrer. These logs exist to serve pages, measure aggregate traffic and block abuse. They are retained for a short period and are not used to build a profile of you.

Cookies and local storage

The reading pages themselves set no cookies. If you arrive from a link with tracking parameters, nothing from that link is stored on your device.

Third-party advertising

If advertising is enabled on this site, third-party vendors — including Google and its partners — may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Where required by law, a consent banner is shown before non-essential cookies are set, and you can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the same banner.

Analytics

If aggregate analytics are used, they record page views and referrers in aggregate form. They are not used to identify individual readers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data about you, and the right to object to the sale or sharing of personal information. Because this site holds no accounts, the data involved is normally limited to server logs and advertising cookies. Write to the address below and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
