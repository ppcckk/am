Chapter 12: Violation Code Zero

# Chapter 12

The stain was three inches from the foundation line when Dale knelt beside it and opened his Field Guide to a blank page.

He didn't write immediately. First he measured.

The black fluid had a leading edge that moved at roughly four inches per minute, and it moved against the grain of the concrete, which meant it was following a moisture path.

He knew this because he'd seen it before in a basement in Cuyahoga Falls, where a failed sump pump had created the same kind of reverse-flow pattern in a different kind of fluid. Different problem. Same principle.

He wrote: VECTOR RESIDUE. MOVING AGAINST SURFACE TENSION. FOLLOWING SUBSTRATE MOISTURE GRADIENT. ESTIMATED PENETRATION TIME TO FOUNDATION LINE: 2 MINUTES.

He had two minutes.

Dale stood up, walked to his truck, and came back with a bag of diatomaceous earth.

Not because it would kill anything. Because it would create a visible barrier that the Authority could verify as a containment attempt.

That was the whole point.

You didn't have to solve the problem in the moment. You had to show you were solving it.

He poured a three-inch band of the white powder across the concrete in front of the stain. The black fluid hit the powder and stopped.

Not permanently, which was already pooling at the edge, building pressure.

But it stopped moving forward for six seconds.

His phone buzzed.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: CONTAINMENT ATTEMPT LOGGED. THOROUGHNESS CONSIDERATION: PARTIAL. NOTE: DIATOMACEOUS EARTH HAS NO TREATMENT VALUE AGAINST CLASS V VECTOR RESIDUE.

CONTAINMENT WILL FAIL WITHIN 90 SECONDS. RECOMMEND IMMEDIATE ELIMINATION PROTOCOL.

He didn't have an elimination protocol, and he had Level I. Level I gave him three tools: Identification, Documentation, and Isolation. Elimination and Verification were Level II.

He was stuck at Isolation.

The stain pushed through the powder in eleven seconds. Faster than the Authority predicted.

Dale went back to his truck. He came back with a roll of contractor-grade plastic sheeting and a box of construction adhesive.

He laid the plastic over the stain and sealed the edges with the adhesive, pressing it flat with his palms.

The stain was still moving under it, and he could see it pushing against the plastic from below, like something breathing.

His phone buzzed.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: CONTAINMENT ATTEMPT LOGGED. THOROUGHNESS CONSIDERATION: PARTIAL.

STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY: 71%.

NOTE: PLASTIC SHEETING WILL NOT PREVENT SUBSTRATE PENETRATION.

VECTOR RESIDUE IS LIQUID. LIQUIDS FOLLOW GRAVITY.

YOUR CONTAINMENT IS COSMETIC.

He knew it was cosmetic. He didn't care.

He'd documented the attempt.

That was worth something. The Authority tracked attempts even when they failed, because attempts proved intent, and intent proved a technician was engaged. A technician who wasn't engaged would have walked away.

He pulled out his phone and opened the job filing interface.

He'd used it twice before. The first time he'd filed the original inspection at Maple Crest.

The second time he'd filed the broodmother elimination at his own apartment.

Both had gone through clean, and he started a new filing. Then he stopped. Deleted it.

He went back to his original job. Job 4412-MAPLE-CREST. The one Vasquez had closed. The one that showed his Thoroughness Rating at 6.1 and his points at zero.

He clicked MODIFY.

The interface didn't expect this. The MODIFY button was grayed out on closed jobs.

But Dale had documented the entry point, and he had the original inspection report.

He had photographs of the foundation crack, timestamped, geotagged, with the violation code K-7 assigned to it. That documentation was his.

It hadn't been reassigned. Vasquez had taken the kill, but the area was still his.

He filed an amendment.

JOB 4412-MAPLE-CREST: AMENDMENT REQUEST. NEW VIOLATION TYPE: VECTOR RESIDUE (CLASS V).

SOURCE: TREATMENT RESIDUE FROM ASSIGNED KILL.

RELATIONSHIP TO ORIGINAL JOB: DIRECT CONSEQUENCE OF DOCUMENTED ENTRY POINT. REQUEST: JOB REOPENED AS CONTINUOUS CASE.

He hit submit, and the Authority took four seconds. That was fast. The Authority was never fast.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: AMENDMENT UNDER REVIEW. NOTE: VECTOR RESIDUE IS A NEW VIOLATION TYPE NOT PREVIOUSLY LOGGED IN YOUR FIELD GUIDE. REVIEWING FOR PRECEDENT.

While he waited, he watched the stain, which had reached the foundation. It was pooling in the gap between the brick and the concrete slab, the same gap he'd documented in his original inspection.

The same gap that had made the house vulnerable to the original rat infestation.

Everything was connected. That was the thing people never understood about pest control. You didn't have a rat problem.

You had a structural problem that rats were exploiting.

His phone buzzed.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: AMENDMENT DENIED.

REASON: VECTOR RESIDUE (CLASS V) EXCEEDS CERTIFICATION SCOPE FOR LEVEL I TECHNICIANS.

JOB REMAINS CLOSED UNDER R. VASQUEZ.

STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY: 64%. RECOMMEND: EVACUATE.

Dale stared at the screen. Then he typed.

COUNTER-ARGUMENT. THE VECTOR RESIDUE IS NOT A NEW INFESTATION.

IT IS A MUTATION OF THE ORIGINAL DOCUMENTED ENTRY POINT (VIOLATION K-7).

THE ORIGINAL ENTRY POINT IS MY DOCUMENTATION. THE MUTATION IS DOWNSTREAM OF MY DOCUMENTATION. THEREFORE THE MUTATION IS CONTINUOUS WITH MY JOB.

I AM NOT REQUESTING PERMISSION TO TREAT A CLASS V THREAT.

I AM REQUESTING PERMISSION TO CONTINUE TREATING MY CLASS I THREAT, WHICH HAS MUTATED DURING THE TREATMENT PROCESS.

He hit send, and the Authority took eleven seconds.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: COUNTER-ARGUMENT LOGGED. REVIEWING FOR PRECEDENT. STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY: 58%.

Dale looked at the house, and the foundation was visibly wet on the north side; the plastic sheeting he'd laid was bubbling. Something underneath it was generating heat.

He could feel it from ten feet away, a warm spot in the air that didn't match the October temperature.

He went back to his truck and grabbed his clipboard.

The physical one.

The Authority's system tracked digital filings, but the Authority also tracked physical documentation because physical documentation proved a technician was present and engaged. He'd learned that in his first week after certification.

Every time he wrote something on paper, the Authority logged it as a "manual verification event" and added a small bonus to his Thoroughness consideration.

He wrote on the clipboard: STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY DECLINING. VECTOR RESIDUE GENERATING THERMAL OUTPUT. ESTIMATED MATURATION WINDOW: 4 HOURS. RECOMMEND: IMMEDIATE TREATMENT OR STRUCTURAL DEMOLITION.

He held the clipboard up to his phone camera. The Authority logged it.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: MANUAL VERIFICATION LOGGED. THOROUGHNESS CONSIDERATION: +0.3. STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY: 51%.

His phone rang, and he answered.

"You're making this complicated," said a voice he didn't recognize. Male. Flat. The kind of voice that had read a lot of regulations and had no patience for people who read them back.

"Who is this?"

"Licensing Inspector Morvath. I'm reviewing your amendment."

"I submitted it through the proper channel."

"You submitted it through a channel that doesn't exist for Level I technicians. You found a workaround. That's not a violation, but it's not a right."

"I have documentation of the original entry point. That documentation is still active. The mutation is downstream of it."

Morvath was quiet for a moment. Dale could hear something in the background.

Not wind.

Not traffic. Something wet. Something that sounded like a hand pressing into mud.

"You're trying to claim a Class V infestation under a Level I job code," Morvath said.

"I'm trying to claim a mutation of a Class I infestation that I documented before it became Class V. The mutation happened during treatment. The treatment was applied to my documented area. My documentation is the through-line."

"That's creative."

"It's accurate."

Another pause. The wet sound in the background got louder.

"I'm going to approve the amendment," Morvath said.

"But I want you to understand what that means. If you claim the job as continuous, you inherit the full mutation chain.

Every stage.

Every escalation. If this thing matures and you haven't eliminated it by the time it reaches its terminal form, your certification gets revoked. Not suspended.

Revoked. You'll be unlicensed.

You'll be a civilian with a clipboard."

"I understand."

"Do you? You're Level I. This is Class V. You don't have the protocols for it."

"I have the protocols for the entry point. The entry point is the problem. If I seal the entry point, the mutation loses its substrate. It starves."

"That's a theory."

"It's a pest control principle. You don't kill the rat. You close the hole. The rat leaves."

Morvath made a sound that might have been a laugh.

"Approved. Job 4412-MAPLE-CREST reopened as continuous case.

Certification scope extended for duration of this job only. You now have access to Level III protocols for this single job. Use them or lose everything."

The call ended.

Dale's phone buzzed.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: CERTIFICATION SCOPE EXTENDED (JOB-SPECIFIC). NEW PROTOCOLS UNLOCKED: STRUCTURAL SEALANT (LEVEL III), MOISTURE REDUCTION TREATMENT (LEVEL III), SUBSTRATE DEPRIVATION (LEVEL III). NOTE: THESE PROTOCOLS ARE UNLOCKED FOR THIS JOB ONLY. USING THEM ON ANY OTHER JOB WILL TRIGGER A CROSS-CONTAMINATION PENALTY AND PERMANENTLY LOCK THEM.

He looked at the house. The plastic sheeting had split. The black fluid was pooling at the base of the north wall, and it was bubbling now. Small bubbles rising to the surface, popping, releasing a smell like burnt hair and copper.

He walked to his truck and opened the back. His kit was there.

The Authority had updated it when the protocols unlocked.

New items appeared in the cargo bed like they'd always been there. A tube of gray sealant that looked like it was made from something that had once been alive. A canister labeled MOISTURE REDUCTION that hissed when he touched it.

A bag of white granules labeled SUBSTRATE DEPRIVATION, and he grabbed the sealant first; it was the most important tool. Not because it would kill anything. Because it would close the hole.

He walked to the north wall. The black fluid was already three inches up the brick.

It was climbing.

Not spreading across surfaces anymore. Climbing them. It had found a vertical path and it was taking it.

Dale opened the sealant tube and applied it to the foundation gap, and the gray substance came out thick and warm.

It expanded as it hit air, filling the gap between the brick and the concrete.

He packed it in with his fingers.

It burned. He kept packing.

His phone buzzed.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: STRUCTURAL SEALANT APPLIED. THOROUGHNESS CONSIDERATION: FULL. STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY: 54%.

IMPROVEMENT LOGGED. NOTE: SEALANT WILL HOLD FOR APPROXIMATELY 6 HOURS.

VECTOR RESIDUE IS GENERATING PRESSURE BEHIND SEALANT.

ESTIMATED BREACH TIME: 5 HOURS 40 MINUTES.

He had six hours. He had three Level III protocols. He had a clipboard and a pen.

He applied the moisture reduction canister next, spraying the interior of the foundation gap before the sealant fully cured.

The canister hissed and the air around the gap went dry. The black fluid on the outside slowed.

It was still climbing, but slower.

It needed moisture. Without moisture, it moved like something with arthritis.

SUBSTRATE DEPRIVATION was the last tool, and he spread the white granules along the base of the wall, inside and out.

They absorbed the black fluid on contact, swelling into a gray paste that clogged the pores in the concrete.

The fluid couldn't find a path anymore, which was trapped between the sealant and the granules, and it was shrinking.

Dale stepped back. He watched. The bubbling slowed.

The climbing stopped. The black stain on the brick was receding, pulling back toward the foundation like a tide going out.

His phone buzzed.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: VECTOR RESIDUE CONTAINED.

STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY: 67%. IMPROVEMENT LOGGED. THOROUGHNESS CONSIDERATION: 8.4.

JOB STATUS: ACTIVE.

NOTE: CONTAINMENT IS TEMPORARY. VECTOR RESIDUE HAS ENTERED DORMANT STATE.

ESTIMATED REACTIVATION: 48 HOURS.

YOU HAVE 48 HOURS TO ELIMINATE THE MUTATION BEFORE IT REACTIVATES AND REACHES MATURATION. IF IT REACHES MATURATION, STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY WILL DROP TO ZERO. THE HOUSE WILL COLLAPSE.

YOU WILL BE HELD LIABLE.

Forty-eight hours. He had a contained problem, not a solved one; the mutation was sleeping under the foundation, and it was going to wake up.

He opened his Field Guide, and he had one entry.

Dimensional Vermin (Rattus profundus). The entry was clean, accurate, and useless against a Class V vector residue.

He needed a new entry. He needed to document the mutation itself, identify its weakness, and write it into the guide before it woke up.

He started writing.

His phone buzzed one more time.

AUTHORITY NOTIFICATION: NEW FIELD GUIDE ENTRY DETECTED (DRAFT). UNVERIFIED.

NOTE: UNVERIFIED ENTRIES DO NOT GRANT DAMAGE BONUS.

VERIFICATION REQUIRES COMBAT ENGAGEMENT WITH THE DOCUMENTED SPECIES. YOU MUST FIGHT IT TO KNOW IT.

Dale looked at the house. The black stain was gone. The foundation was sealed. The house looked normal.

It was going to wake up in forty-eight hours, and the only way to learn how to kill it was to be inside the house when it did.

He picked up his clipboard and started writing a new violation code.

The code had no name yet.
