Certified Exterminator

LitRPG · 41 chapters · 90,547 words

Progression FantasyComedyUrban FantasyMonster Hunting

Dale Kowalski has been spraying for ants, sealing entry points, and writing inspection reports for fourteen years. He knows the difference between a German cockroach and a brown-banded cockroach. He knows what a frass trail looks like. He knows that if you don't treat the nest, the problem comes back in six weeks.

When the Rift opens over suburban Ohio and things start crawling out of the ground, Dale doesn't panic. He pulls out his clipboard.

The Authority—a bureaucratic entity that predates human civilization—recognizes his instincts immediately. His decade of professional pest management translates directly into a combat system built around identification, documentation, and targeted elimination. He doesn't get a sword. He gets a Field Guide, a protocol toolkit, and a certification track that rewards the one thing no hero in this world has ever bothered to do: thoroughness.

The other survivors fight monsters. Dale exterminates them.

And the Authority has a nine-level licensing structure, a growing backlog of untreated infestations, and a Thoroughness Rating that will determine whether he ever gets to deal with whatever is actually crawling out of the Rift.
