About Serial Shelf

Serial Shelf is a small independent serial-fiction shelf. It publishes long-form English web serials — currently two: Up to Code (Dungeon Core) and Certified Exterminator (LitRPG) — with new chapters on a fixed schedule.

What we publish

Both serials are built on the same joke and take it seriously: bureaucracy as a magic system. A municipal building inspector who becomes a dungeon core can shut a rival dungeon down by citing it for code violations. A pest-control technician discovers that monsters are infestations with a higher certification level, and that the licensing system rewards thoroughness over heroism. The humour comes from procedure; the stakes come from what procedure costs the people inside it.

Every chapter is edited for continuity against the previous chapters before publication, and each book is readable start to finish without an account, an email address or a paywall.

How it is made

Serial Shelf is written and edited by a human editor working with drafting tools, in the same way a small magazine works with a word processor. Drafts are generated, then cut, reordered, and rewritten for voice, pacing, and continuity before they appear here. The editor is responsible for everything published on this site.

Reading

Read online chapter by chapter, subscribe to the RSS feed, or download the complete EPUB of either book from its index page. Chapters are free; there is no paywall and no locked chapter.
